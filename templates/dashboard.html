AIMI Settings (used by the recommendations engine)
Why fill this in? Enter your current AIMI values here so the app can compare them against what it recommends. This is what fills the "currently X" in the checklist, the grey current-marker on each slider, and the change highlighting. Without it, the app only sees your glucose data and profile — it can suggest targets but can't tell you which of your settings need changing.
📁 Import from AndroidAPS settings file
Fastest, most accurate way to fill these in. In AAPS: Maintenance → Export settings. Choose your export file or paste its contents. If the export is encrypted, enter your AAPS master password below so it can be read.
